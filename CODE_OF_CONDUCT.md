# Code of Conduct

ARSVIN is built for protection engineers, substation automation engineers, researchers, students, and open-source maintainers.

Participants are expected to be respectful, technical, constructive, and safety-conscious.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain engineering reasoning clearly.
- Respect that contributors may have different field experience levels.
- Keep safety warnings honest and visible.
- Avoid sharing confidential station data or production network information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or repeated hostile comments
- Publishing private contact details or confidential engineering data
- Encouraging unauthorized network testing or unsafe live substation use
- Spam, unrelated promotion, or intentionally misleading claims

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may lead to blocking from the project.
